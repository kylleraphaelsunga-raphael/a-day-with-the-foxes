About the Event

Event highlights, details

A Day with the Foxes is a special event that welcomes students to experience the vibrant community of Holy Angel University. It gives participants the opportunity to explore the campus, join exciting activities, and learn more about student life.

The event is designed to help students discover the academic programs, organizations, and opportunities available at HAU. It is also a great chance to meet fellow students, faculty members, and the Fox family. Join us for a fun and memorable day as you truly experience what it means to be part of the Fox community.

Event Highlights

  • Experience a day as a Fox at Holy Angel University
  • Explore the HAU campus and its facilities
  • Learn about HAU's academic programs and student organizations
  • Participate in interactive games, activities, and campus tours
  • Meet faculty members, students, and fellow future Angelites
  • Enjoy exciting giveaways and memorable experiences
